## Monthly Trading Rules – Profit & Loss Circuit Breakers

### 1. Monthly Profit Target Hit
- Once the monthly profit target is achieved, reduce position size to **50% of normal risk per trade** for the remainder of the month.
- Do not return to full position size during that month.
- Protect the profits already earned. Do not become aggressive just because the month is profitable.

### 2. Monthly Loss Limit Hit
- Once the monthly loss limit is reached or breached, **STOP ALL LIVE TRADING immediately** for the remainder of the month.
- Cancel all pending entry orders.
- Do not open new positions or attempt to recover losses.
- Manage existing positions strictly according to predefined stop-loss rules.
- Resume normal trading only at the beginning of the next calendar month, after reviewing the previous month's performance.

### 3. Learning Never Stops – Even When Trading Stops
- Continue updating the daily watchlist and maintaining charts every trading day.
- Identify potential setups, mark entry levels, stop-losses and targets.
- Observe how shortlisted stocks behave and whether the setups meet trading rules.
- Record hypothetical trades and track their outcomes.
- Review missed opportunities, failed breakouts and successful setups.
- Continue improving the strategy through observation, analysis and journaling.

**Stopping live trading does not mean stopping the learning process.**

### 4. Non-Negotiable Discipline
- No discretionary overrides of monthly circuit breakers.
- No revenge trading or emotional decisions.
- Never increase risk to recover losses.
- Separate trading activity from learning and research activity.

**Core Principle: Protect capital. Preserve discipline. Never stop learning.**
