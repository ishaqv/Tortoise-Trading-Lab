## When to Switch to an Extended Target or Partial Exit

### The Problem

At entry, we don't know whether a trade will produce a normal move or an exceptional momentum move.

If we always exit 100% at T1, we may leave a large part of a strong move on the table.

But if we always keep a runner, price may hit T1, fail to continue, reverse, and eventually hit the original SL. In that case, taking 100% off at T1 would have been better.

So the goal is not to always let winners run.

**The goal is to identify exceptional momentum early enough to justify giving the trade more room.**

### The Solution

Use the **primary timeframe for structure** and a **lower timeframe for early momentum confirmation**.

**Timeframe structure:**

> **Primary TF = 5 min**
> **Momentum TF = 2 min**

The 5-minute chart remains responsible for the setup, entry and overall trade structure.

After entry, use the 2-minute chart as an **early momentum detector**.

Look for:

**Primary TF — 5 min**

* Price making Higher Highs
* Strong continuation
* Price holding above the breakout/confirmation area

**Momentum TF — 2 min**

* Volume significantly above average — for example, **>2–3× average volume**
* Consecutive Higher Highs
* Strong directional candles
* Shallow pullbacks
* Continued momentum toward T1

When these signals align, the trade can be classified as an **Exceptional Momentum Trade**.

### Exit Decision

**Normal momentum →** Take the planned T1 and exit normally.

**Exceptional momentum →** Consider either:

* **Extending the target** and keeping the full position, or
* **Taking a partial exit at T1** and allowing the remaining position to run.

The decision should be made **as price approaches T1**, using the information already available from both timeframes.

### Core Principle

> **Fixed T1 is the default. Extended target or partial exit is earned by evidence of exceptional momentum.**

We are not trying to predict a big move.

We are asking:

> **"Is the evidence strong enough to justify giving the trade more room?"**

If yes, allow more room.

If no, **take T1 and protect the profit.**

The objective is to protect ordinary winners while giving exceptional trades the opportunity to become exceptional winners.
