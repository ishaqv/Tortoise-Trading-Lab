# 📘 Trading Playbook

> **Mission:** Protect capital. Follow the process. Let probabilities work over a series of trades.

---

# 🎯 Core Principles

* Protect capital first.
* Risk a fixed amount (1R) per trade.
* Accept small losses.
* Let winners run.
* Follow the plan, not emotions.
* Judge yourself by **process**, not P&L.

---

# 🚀 10-Step Trade Execution Process

## 1. Trade Only Valid Setups

* Never force a trade.
* Wait patiently for a valid setup.
* **No Setup = No Trade.**

---

## 2. Place Entry (SL-M Order)

* Place the SL-M entry order at the predefined level.
* Verify:

  * Quantity
  * Entry price
  * Direction (Long/Short)
  * Product type

---

## 3. Place Emergency Stop-Loss

Immediately after the entry order is triggered:

* Place a wide emergency stop-loss.
* Protect against entry-candle volatility and unexpected moves.
* **Maximum loss is limited to 2R.**

---

## 4. Place Target (Limit Order)

* Place the target limit order immediately.
* Never trade without both a stop-loss and a target.

---

## 5. Replace with Normal SL After Candle Close

After the entry candle closes:

* Replace the emergency SL with the normal SL.
* If price closes beyond the normal SL:

  * Below it for **Long**
  * Above it for **Short**
* Exit immediately.

**Don't hope. Don't wait.**

---

## 6. Move SL to Breakeven

Move the stop-loss to breakeven **only after predefined conditions are met** (e.g., price reaches **2R**).

* Never move the stop early out of fear.
* Protect capital before maximizing profits.

---

## 7. Manage Winners (Trail / Partial Exit)

Consider extending the target or taking a partial exit **only if**:

* Volume is **well above average across multiple candles**.
* Price maintains:

  * Higher High → Higher Low (Long)
  * Lower Low → Lower High (Short)

Ignore Market Depth. It is often unreliable.

---

## 8. Cancel Remaining Orders

When either the stop-loss or target is hit:

* Cancel all remaining pending orders.
* Keep the order book clean.

---

## 9. Trade Postmortem

After market close:

Review every trade.

Record:

* What went right
* What went wrong
* Rule violations
* Emotional mistakes
* Lessons learned

---

## 10. Review Weekly & Monthly

**Weekly**

* Review behavior.
* Identify recurring mistakes.
* Measure rule adherence.

**Monthly**

* Review performance metrics.
* Make changes only with sufficient data.
* Never modify the strategy based on one or two trades.

---

# 🛡 Risk Rules

* Risk only **1R** per trade.
* Emergency stop-loss limits the maximum loss to **2R**.
* Never widen a stop-loss.
* Never average down.
* Never revenge trade.
* When in doubt, stay out.

---

# ✅ Daily Checklist

### Before Market

* ☐ Mark key levels.
* ☐ Review market context.
* ☐ Calculate position size.
* ☐ Wait for a valid setup.

### During Trade

* ☐ Entry placed.
* ☐ Emergency SL placed.
* ☐ Target placed.
* ☐ Normal SL activated.
* ☐ Breakeven rule followed.
* ☐ Winner managed according to the plan.

### After Trade

* ☐ Cancel remaining orders.
* ☐ Take chart screenshots.
* ☐ Complete the trade journal.
