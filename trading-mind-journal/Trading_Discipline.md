# Trading Discipline

> **Before you start trading, learn to be disciplined in life.**

Trading magnifies your existing habits.

If you are impulsive in life, you will be impulsive in trading.  
If you struggle to follow rules in life, you will eventually break your trading rules.  
If you seek instant gratification, the market will constantly tempt you.

And unlike ordinary life, **one moment of indiscipline in trading can cost a large amount of money.**

So the first question is not:

> *"Do I have a profitable strategy?"*

It is:

> **"Am I disciplined enough to follow my strategy?"**

### First build discipline in life.

Learn to:

- Do what you said you would do.
- Control impulses.
- Delay gratification.
- Follow routines even when you don't feel like it.
- Accept discomfort without immediately escaping it.
- Stop making exceptions for yourself.
- Take responsibility for your decisions.
- Stay consistent when motivation disappears.

Then bring that discipline into trading.

Because a profitable strategy in the hands of an undisciplined trader can become a losing strategy.

> **First discipline yourself.  
> Then trade.**

And once you start trading:

> **No setup = No trade.  
> No exceptions.**
